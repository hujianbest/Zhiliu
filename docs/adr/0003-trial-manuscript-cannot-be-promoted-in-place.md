# A Trial Manuscript can never become Formal in place

Trial Manuscripts are speculative output the user never asked for, and they must stay separate from formal articles until the user has confirmed a thesis and the material behind it. In-place promotion is the obvious convenience feature and is also a back door around that confirmation, so a Trial Manuscript can only pre-fill a Proposal; confirming the Proposal produces a new Formal Manuscript, and the Trial is kept as history.

## Considered Options

- **A "promote to formal" action that flips the kind and records where the text came from** was rejected. It satisfies the audit requirement but not the product one: the user would end up owning an article whose thesis they never confirmed, and the recorded origin would be the only thing standing between them and claiming AI-chosen argument as their own. The confirmation step is not a paper trail, it is the moment the argument becomes the user's.
- **Blocking Trial Manuscripts from touching formal writing at all** was rejected for the opposite reason: it throws away the Agent's most useful output and pushes the user to copy text across by hand, which loses provenance entirely.

## Consequences

- Turning a good trial draft into a real article always costs the user one review step, which is the point rather than an oversight.
- Two manuscripts exist afterwards, so the Creation surface must make the relationship between them visible instead of looking like a duplicate.
